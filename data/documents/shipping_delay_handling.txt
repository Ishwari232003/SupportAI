DELAYED SHIPMENT HANDLING GUIDE

Policy Name: Delayed Shipment Handling Guide
Version: 1
Effective Date: 2025-06-01
Document Type: Shipping Guidelines

1. When a Shipment Is Delayed
Status delayed means the expected date has passed. Check the delay reason in the shipment record.

2. Agent Actions
1. Tell the customer the reason and the new expected date.
2. Contact the courier escalation contact if the delay exceeds 3 days.
3. Raise a ticket for the Order Management Executive.

3. Goodwill Rules
- 3 or more days past the expected date: offer a goodwill coupon of Rs 100.
- 7 or more days past the expected date: offer cancellation with a full refund.

4. Region South Note
South region has the highest delay rate. Hub congestion is the most common reason. Use the proactive delay message template.
