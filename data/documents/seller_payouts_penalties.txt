SELLER PAYOUTS AND PENALTIES

Policy Name: Seller Payouts and Penalties
Version: 1
Effective Date: 2025-06-01
Document Type: Seller Guidelines

1. Payout Cycle
Payouts are made 7 days after successful delivery.

2. Deductions
Commission, shipping fees and refund amounts for returns are deducted from payouts.

3. Penalties
- Late dispatch beyond 48 hours: Rs 50 per order.
- Seller cancellation: Rs 100 per order.
- Counterfeit product: account suspension.
