PAYMENT DEDUCTED BUT ORDER NOT PLACED

Policy Name: Payment Deducted but Order Not Placed
Version: 1
Effective Date: 2025-06-01
Document Type: Troubleshooting Guide

1. Check
Confirm the payment status in the order record.

2. Action
Tell the customer the amount is reversed automatically in 5 to 7 business days.

3. Escalate
If more than 7 business days have passed, escalate to the Team Lead.
