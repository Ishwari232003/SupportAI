SOP: REFUND INVESTIGATION

Policy Name: SOP: Refund Investigation
Version: 1
Effective Date: 2025-06-01
Document Type: Customer Support SOP

1. Purpose
Steps for customers whose refund is late.

2. Steps
1. Look up the refund record and status.
2. Read the delay reason.
3. Follow the action for that reason in the Refund Policy.
4. Escalate if pending for more than 7 business days.
