ELECTRONICS PRODUCT FAQ

Policy Name: Electronics Product FAQ
Version: 1
Effective Date: 2025-06-01
Document Type: Product FAQ

1. What is the warranty period for Electronics products?
12 months from delivery.

2. How many days do I have to return a Electronics product?
10 days from delivery under Return Policy Version 6.

3. How do I claim warranty or raise a return?
Raise a support ticket with the order ID. The agent verifies delivery date and eligibility.

4. How long does a refund take?
Within 7 business days after the returned item passes quality check.

5. Can I exchange instead of returning?
Exchange is available for Defective and Size issue reasons, subject to stock.
