REFUND ESCALATION PROCEDURE

Policy Name: Refund Escalation Procedure
Version: 1
Effective Date: 2025-06-01
Document Type: Escalation Procedure

1. Trigger
A refund is pending for more than 7 business days.

2. Actions
1. Check the refund delay reason: Bank processing delay, Return QC pending or Payment gateway failure.
2. For Return QC pending, ask the Warehouse Manager to complete QC in 24 hours.
3. For Payment gateway failure, ask the Refunds team to re-initiate the refund.
4. If still unresolved after 15 calendar days, escalate to the Operations Manager.
