CASH ON DELIVERY POLICY

Policy Name: Cash on Delivery Policy
Version: 1
Effective Date: 2025-06-01
Document Type: Payment Policy

1. COD Availability
COD is available for orders up to Rs 50,000 in serviceable pin codes.

2. Refunds for COD
COD refunds are paid to a verified bank account within 10 business days of QC.

3. COD Fraud Prevention
Customers with 3 or more refused COD deliveries lose COD access.
