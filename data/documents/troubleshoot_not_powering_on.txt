PRODUCT NOT POWERING ON

Policy Name: Product Not Powering On
Version: 1
Effective Date: 2025-06-01
Document Type: Troubleshooting Guide

1. Customer Steps
1. Charge the device for 30 minutes using the original cable.
2. Hold the power button for 10 seconds to reset.
3. Try a different socket or cable.

2. If It Still Fails
Within the return window, raise a return with reason Defective. After the return window, raise a warranty claim.

3. Example
Product X: 10-day return window, 12-month warranty.
