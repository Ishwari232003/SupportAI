KB: DELAYED SHIPMENTS IN THE SOUTH REGION

Policy Name: KB: Delayed Shipments in the South Region
Version: 1
Effective Date: 2025-06-01
Document Type: Knowledge Base Article

1. Summary
South region shows the highest share of delayed shipments.

2. Main Causes
Hub congestion, weather disruption (monsoon) and address issues.

3. What Agents Should Do
Use the proactive delay template and follow the Delayed Shipment Handling Guide.
