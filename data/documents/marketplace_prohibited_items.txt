PROHIBITED ITEMS POLICY

Policy Name: Prohibited Items Policy
Version: 1
Effective Date: 2025-06-01
Document Type: Marketplace Policy

1. Prohibited Items
Weapons, drugs, counterfeit goods, stolen goods and hazardous chemicals are banned.

2. Restricted Items
Prescription medicines and imported electronics need documents before listing.

3. Action
Violating listings are removed and the seller may be suspended.
