HOME PRODUCTS WARRANTY POLICY

Policy Name: Home Products Warranty Policy
Version: 1
Effective Date: 2025-06-01
Document Type: Warranty Document

1. Warranty Period
Home products carry a manufacturer warranty of 6 months from the date of delivery.

2. What Is Covered
Manufacturing defects and component failures under normal use. Repair or replacement is at the discretion of the authorized service center.

3. What Is Not Covered
Physical or liquid damage, unauthorized repairs, misuse, normal wear and tear, and damage caused by power surges.

4. How to Claim Warranty
1. Keep the invoice and the order ID.
2. Raise a support ticket with the subject Warranty claim.
3. The agent verifies the delivery date and warranty period in the order record.
4. The customer is directed to an authorized service center or a replacement is arranged.

5. Product-Specific Notes
Home products carry a 6-month warranty. Cosmetic wear is excluded.

6. Warranty vs Return
Warranty is separate from the return window. The return window for Home is 15 days from delivery; after that, only warranty claims apply.
